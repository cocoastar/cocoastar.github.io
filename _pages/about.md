I am Jiangyang Li (李江洋), a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the MIV Lab, Xi'an Jiaotong University (2024–2027), advised by Prof. Yihong Gong. I received my Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2024.

My research interests include Foundation Models, Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), Reinforcement Learning, and World Models.
